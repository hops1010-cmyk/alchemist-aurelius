The map was not just a map. The map was a code. And the code was not just a code. The quarks split. They split the way atoms split — with energy, with force, with the release of something that had been held. The quarks split, and the splitting made energy, and the energy was the power, and the power was the Tower, and the Tower was the system, and the system was whole.

The quarks split in the gap. In the 2.5 seconds. In the space between the start and the end, where time did not move but remembered. In that gap, the quarks split, and the splitting made energy, and the energy was the power that ran the Tower, and the Tower was the system, and the system was whole.

Aurelius understood this. The oscillator regulated the time. It regulated the backward loop. It regulated the gap. It regulated the memory. It regulated the quarks. It regulated the splitting. It regulated the energy. It regulated the Tower. It regulated the system. It regulated the whole.

The oscillator was in the map. The map was in the phone. The phone was in Pete's hand. Pete's hand was on the lanai. The lanai was in Hawaii. Hawaii was 8,000 miles from the Tower. The distance was zero.
