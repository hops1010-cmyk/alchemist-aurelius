## Back Page: To the Reader

Did you discover what the pin key?

The pin keys were the clues. The clues were the gems. The gems were the parchment. The parchment was the special. The special was the paper. The paper was the network. The network was the system. The system was whole.

If you found them — in the maze, in the tunnel, in the inn, on Lester street, at the stables, on the forest trail, at the lake, in the boat, on the hill, on the battlefield, in the message, in the scrolls — then you already know.

If you didn't — look again. The pin keys open the doors. The doors are the maze. The maze is the map. The map is the board. The board is the game. The game is the system. The system is whole.

Build it right. Build it strong. Build it to last.

— The Author
