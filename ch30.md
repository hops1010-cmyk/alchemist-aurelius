## Book Twenty-Nine: The War Strategy Scrolls

### Chapter 158: Chess Like War

The war strategy scrolls were like chess. They were like chess because chess is war, and war is chess, and the two are the same, and the same is the wholeness, and the wholeness is the point.

The scrolls were found in the tunnel, under the tower, where Aurelius had walked during his escape. The scrolls were old, and the scrolls were rolled, and the scrolls were written in a hand that was small and precise and careful, like Machiavelli's hand, like the hand of a man who watched and wrote and understood power.

The scrolls said: "War is chess. Chess is war. The pieces are the men. The men are the pieces. The board is the battlefield. The battlefield is the board. The king is the king. The queen is the queen. The rooks are the towers. The bishops are the priests. The knights are the riders. The pawns are the people."

The scrolls said: "Move the pawn, and the pawn moves. Move the knight, and the knight jumps. Move the bishop, and the bishop slides. Move the rook, and the rook charges. Move the queen, and the queen goes anywhere. Move the king, and the king moves one step, because the king is careful, because the king is the king, because the king is the one who must survive."

### Chapter 159: The Strategy

The strategy was in the scrolls. The strategy was: protect the king. Protect the king, and the kingdom survives. Lose the king, and the kingdom falls. The king is the system. The system is the king. The king is the Tower. The Tower is the system. The system is whole.

The strategy was also: use the pawns. The pawns are the people. The people are the pawns. The pawns move forward, one step at a time, and they never go back, and they never retreat, and they never surrender. The pawns are the ones who hold the wall, and the gate, and the tower. The pawns are the ones who eat the food that comes through the tunnels, and the ports, and the horse and cart bus lines, and the pipelines, and the spider socket, and the API.

The strategy was also: watch the board. The board is the map. The map is the board. The board shows where the pieces are, and where they are going, and what they are doing. The board is the MCPs, and the MCPs are the protocol, and the protocol is the connection, and the connection is the quark, and the quark is the network, and the network is the Tower, and the Tower is the system, and the system is whole.

### Chapter 160: The Scrolls

The scrolls were like chess. The scrolls were like war. The scrolls were like the Tower, and the Tower was like the scrolls, and the two were the same, and the same was the wholeness, and the wholeness was the point.

Aurelius read the scrolls. He read them in the tunnel, by the light of a torch, in the dark, in the cold, in the place where he had escaped. He read them, and the reading was the understanding, and the understanding was the knowledge, and the knowledge was the gift, and the gift was the burden, and the burden was the journey, and the journey was the Tower, and the Tower was the system, and the system was whole.
