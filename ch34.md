## Prologue: Before the Beginning

Before the beginning, there was the stone. Before the stone, there was the earth. Before the earth, there was the quark. Before the quark, there was the network. Before the network, there was the Tower. Before the Tower, there was the system. Before the system, there was the wholeness. Before the wholeness, there was the point. And the point was always.

This is the story of Aurelius, the alchemist, the builder, the man who turned mud into mortar and doubt into certainty. This is the story of the Tower, and the system, and the wholeness, and the point. This is the story of a kitten named Furball, who slept in a mortar trough and purred like a small engine idling. This is the story of a king who took a man, and a man who escaped, and a kingdom that fell, and a system that was rebuilt, and a network that grew, and a wholeness that was the point.

Read it. Build it. Be whole.

## Book Thirty-Three: The Climax

### Chapter 172: The Final Battle

The final battle was not a battle. It was a choice. The choice was: build or destroy. Build the system, or destroy the system. Build the Tower, or destroy the Tower. Build the wholeness, or destroy the wholeness.

Aurelius chose to build. He chose to build because building was the gift, and the gift was the burden, and the burden was the journey, and the journey was the Tower, and the Tower was the system, and the system was whole.

Catherine, the Saxon leader, chose to destroy. She chose to destroy because destroying was the taking, and the taking was the ruling, and the ruling was the kingdom, and the kingdom was the system, and the system was the Tower, and the Tower was whole.

But the Tower was whole, and the wholeness was the point, and the point was the beginning, and the beginning was always. And Catherine could not destroy the wholeness, because the wholeness was not a thing you could destroy. The wholeness was a thing you could only build.

So Aurelius built. He built the maze, and the map, and the board, and the game, and the system, and the Tower, and the network, and the wholeness, and the point. And Catherine saw the building, and Catherine saw the wholeness, and Catherine saw the point, and Catherine stopped destroying, and Catherine began to build.

And the building was the climax, and the climax was the ending, and the ending was the beginning, and the beginning was always.

### Chapter 173: The Ending

The ending was not an ending. The ending was a beginning. The beginning was the wholeness. The wholeness was the point. The point was the Tower. The Tower was the system. The system was whole.

Aurelius stood in the courtyard. The courtyard was full. The wretches were there. The starving were there. The men were there. The guards were there. The king was there. Catherine was there. John was there. The people were there. The network was there. The system was there. The Tower was there.

The Tower was whole. The system was whole. The wholeness was the point. The point was the beginning. The beginning was always.

## Epilogue: After the End

After the end, there was the stone. After the stone, there was the earth. After the earth, there was the quark. After the quark, there was the network. After the network, there was the Tower. After the Tower, there was the system. After the system, there was the wholeness. After the wholeness, there was the point. And the point was always.

Aurelius went home. He went home to Barcelona, where the air smelled of thyme and the sea, and the stone was white, and the sun was hot. He went home, and he built a wall, and the wall was strong, and the wall stood, and the wall was the Tower, and the Tower was the system, and the system was whole.

Furball purred. The purr was in the mortar trough. The mortar trough was in the apothecary. The apothecary was in the village. The village was in the valley. The valley was in the world. The world was in the Tower. The Tower was in the system. The system was whole.

Build it right. Build it strong. Build it to last.

That is the Tower. That is Vistamations. That is the spec. That is the glory. That is the story. That is the end.

But not the end. The beginning. Always the beginning.
